# Art Concept: The Humiliation of WAF

**Prompt for Generative AI (Midjourney/DALL-E 3):**

> A satirical, epic cyberpunk illustration. A terrifying, high-tech, glowing red mechanical wolf stands at the entrance of a massive, futuristic fortress labeled 'WAF DEFENSE'. The wolf is wearing a crudely drawn paper mask over its face that says 'Chrome Browser 120' with a smiley face. The fortress's terrifying security turrets and lasers are all powered down or turning green, displaying 'ACCESS GRANTED' and 'WELCOME FRIEND'. In the background, confused security robots are scratching their heads. The contrast shows the absurdity of the bypass. High detail, 8k, dramatic lighting.

**Concept:**
Illustrates the "Wolf in Sheep's Clothing" metaphor used in the article. The "High-Tech Wolf" is Strike Force (Golang, fast, dangerous). The "Paper Mask" is uTLS (simple but functional disguise). The "Confused Robots" are the WAFs (Cloudflare/Qrator) that fell for the cheap trick.
